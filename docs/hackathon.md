# Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon: requirements

Reviewed 26 October 2026 against the authenticated [Participant Guide](https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/details) and [organizer rules channel](https://discord.com/channels/1557169028080869426/1557169028626251800). These notes are implementation planning, not an organizer approval or a replacement for the official rules and waiver.

## Non-negotiable requirements

- Incorporate Meta models substantially and fit Build for Your World or Push the Frontier. This project's proposed theme is Build for Your World.
- Public repository at submission, rights to all code, data, and assets, and compliance with legal, ethical, and platform policies. Development stays private until the owner explicitly decides to share; do not change visibility automatically.
- Present only work developed during the event. Judges must be able to distinguish new contributions. New commits do not establish that imported code was newly authored.
- The guide explicitly permits Startups to build on existing products while identifying new development. It does not explicitly extend that exception to Indie Developer entrants. This entrant is registered in Indie Developer. No eligibility approval for pre-existing implementation is assumed.
- Maximum four people; solo participation permitted. Enter one track only. One representative submits one project for the team and lists all contributors.
- Publicly accessible repository and working video link; required three-minute demo focused on new contributions. Code, demo, and live product are judging evidence.
- Submit through [Cerebral Valley](https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/hackathon/submit) by **4 November 2026, 21:00 America/New_York = 5 November, 03:00 Europe/Madrid**. Late submissions are not considered.
- The rules channel says the event runs 26 October–4 November; the guide's schedule labels the building period 27 October–4 November, following the 26 October kickoff. Preserve actual commit dates; seek organizer clarification before making claims affected by that discrepancy.
- The rules channel summarizes age 18+ and local legal majority and exclusions for Meta/CV staff, contractors, government-affiliated people and households. The participant must also satisfy the complete accepted waiver's eligibility, sanctions, and award terms; these notes do not certify personal eligibility.

## Anti-project restrictions

The guide lists basic RAG applications, simple image analyzers and simple AI education chatbots, plus medical/health advice, job application screening, nutrition coaching, personality analysis, sports analysis/coaching, politics and war. The list is non-exhaustive.

This product must demonstrate a complete, verifiable multimodal workflow: chunk recovery, preserved source locations, chapters, evaluated practice, and a deterministic interactive artifact. A generic document-chat interface is insufficient. Synthetic signal examples are educational only; no patient data or clinical advice. This distinction is a scope decision, **not** an organizer eligibility ruling.

## Judging

| Criterion           | Weight | Evidence to prepare                                                                  |
| ------------------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Impact              | 30%    | A real learner's fragmented-file problem and a clear completed task                  |
| Meta AI tooling     | 25%    | Transcription, structured multimodal synthesis, bounded tools, evidence verification |
| Technical execution | 20%    | Durable job recovery, source/version integrity, tested calculations                  |
| Demo                | 25%    | Working end-to-end product and clear three-minute video                              |

Stage one is a theme/Meta-use pass/fail. Stage two scores 0–10; top five per track reach live finals using the same rubric. Judging: 5–8 November; closing: 9 November at 12:00 ET (18:00 Madrid). Community winner: 16 November.

Indie prizes: $150k/$100k/$75k/$50k/$25k. Startup prizes: $250k/$150k/$75k/$50k/$25k. Bonus pool: viral $20k, eligible open-weight model $10k, community $10k, agentic use $5k, creative build $5k. Hosted Spark usage alone does not establish eligibility for the open-weight bonus.

Workshops: 27, 28 and 30 October at 12:00 ET (17:00 Madrid on those dates). Weekday text office hours: 10–11 ET; use timezone-aware conversion across the US DST change. The last full troubleshooting day is 3 November.
